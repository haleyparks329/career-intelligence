# Person-Model Safety

- Do not invent experience, impact, credentials, or ownership.
- Keep reflection separate from independently supported work evidence.
- Do not turn preferences or personality interpretations into facts.
- Preserve conflicts, gaps, and cautions.
- Distinguish ability to perform a role from likelihood of thriving in an environment.
- Require human review before a claim becomes public career material.

The rules above are principles, not an executable policy or prompt.
