# Public Architecture

Career Intelligence separates evidence, claims, comparisons, and generated language.

- Evidence records what a source can support.
- Claims summarize evidence while preserving uncertainty and allowed use.
- Opportunity comparison asks whether the available evidence supports a requirement.
- Environment considerations remain separate from demonstrated capability.
- A human decides what may be published or emphasized.

The public case study intentionally omits the complete data model, ranking, configuration, policy, generation, and acquisition layers.
