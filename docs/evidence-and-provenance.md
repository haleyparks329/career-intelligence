# Evidence and Provenance

A career claim is only as trustworthy as the path back to its source.

The public example records a short source label, a fictional observation, the capability it may support, and a caution. This is enough to demonstrate the reasoning boundary without publishing the full private model.

Evidence strength is contextual. A work sample may support that something was produced; it may not prove sole ownership, business impact, or a general trait. Reflection can help a person examine preferences, but it cannot silently become an accomplishment or credential.
